How to Play

  • Hiders: Move with WASD/Arrows. Stand in a spot and press Spacebar (or drag mouse) to paint the background onto your body. Hide from the seeker!
  • Seekers: Wait for the hiding phase to end. Scan the background and click where you suspect a hider is hiding. But beware: there is a 2-second cooldown on clicks!
